# finance-variance

## Instructions

1. Start with the measured variance: current period, prior period, absolute variance, and percentage variance.
2. Separate favorable and unfavorable movement. Revenue increases are favorable; expense increases are unfavorable; net income decreases are unfavorable.
3. Explain the top drivers in descending absolute variance order.
4. Tie driver labels to business causes such as new customer launch, churn, product mix, cloud usage, headcount, bonus, or one-time vendor bill.
5. Do not make a claim unless a SQL evidence row or lineage node supports it.
6. End each finding with a confidence level and any limitation.
